# Artery Capital - Application Scoring & Valuation System

## Overview

The Artery Capital Application Scoring System is a comprehensive evaluation framework that combines three world-class startup evaluation methodologies:

1. **Y Combinator Principles** - Focus on team, traction, and product-market fit
2. **Silicon Valley Criteria** - Emphasis on scalability, disruption, and market size
3. **Harambeans Philosophy** - African context, impact, and sustainability

The system automatically evaluates startup applications and generates:
- Overall score (0-100)
- Category-level scoring breakdown
- Current valuation
- 3-year and 5-year projected valuations
- Strengths and concerns analysis
- Actionable next steps

## Evaluation Framework

### Scoring Categories & Weights

| Category | Weight | Framework | Focus Area |
|----------|--------|-----------|------------|
| Founder Quality | 20% | Y Combinator | Team credentials, experience, commitment |
| Traction | 18% | Y Combinator | Revenue, users, growth metrics |

## Detailed Scoring Criteria

### 1. Founder Quality (20% weight)

**What we evaluate:**
- LinkedIn presence (30 points)
- Email professionalism (20 points)
- Team size and composition (30 points)
- Contact information completeness (20 points)

**Why it matters:**
Y Combinator famously says "We invest in people, not ideas." A strong, committed team with relevant experience is the #1 predictor of startup success.

**Scoring logic:**
- **LinkedIn**: Full profile with company domain = 30 pts, partial = 15 pts
- **Email**: Custom domain = 20 pts (shows commitment), free email = 10 pts
- **Team Size**: 10+ people = 30 pts, 5-9 = 25 pts, 3-4 = 20 pts, 2 = 15 pts, solo = 5 pts
- **Completeness**: Full info (name, phone, email, LinkedIn) = 20 pts

---

### 2. Traction (18% weight)

**What we evaluate:**
- Revenue metrics (40 points)
- User/customer base (30 points)
- Growth rate (30 points)

**Why it matters:**
Traction is the best signal of product-market fit. Actual metrics beat projections every time. Y Combinator looks for 10% week-over-week growth.

**Scoring logic:**
- **Revenue**:
  - $100k+ = 40 pts
  - $50k-$100k = 35 pts
  - $10k-$50k = 30 pts
  - $1k-$10k = 20 pts
  - Pre-revenue but honest = 5 pts
- **Users**:
  - 100k+ = 30 pts
  - 50k-100k = 25 pts
  - 10k-50k = 20 pts
  - 1k-10k = 15 pts
  - <1k = 10 pts
- **Growth**:
  - 10%+ weekly = 30 pts
  - 20%+ monthly = 30 pts
  - 100%+ yearly = 20 pts

---

### 3. Product-Market Fit (15% weight)

**What we evaluate:**
- Problem clarity and depth (35 points)
- Solution quality and differentiation (35 points)
- Impact articulation (30 points)

**Why it matters:**
Marc Andreessen says PMF is "being in a good market with a product that can satisfy that market." Clear articulation of problem-solution fit is essential.

**Scoring logic:**
- **Problem Statement**:
  - 200+ chars with specific examples = 35 pts
  - 100-200 chars = 25 pts
  - 50-100 chars = 15 pts
- **Solution**:
  - 200+ chars with differentiation = 35 pts
  - 100-200 chars = 25 pts
  - 50-100 chars = 15 pts
- **Impact**:
  - 200+ chars with measurable outcomes = 30 pts
  - 100-200 chars = 20 pts
  - 50-100 chars = 10 pts

---

### 4. Market Opportunity (15% weight)

**What we evaluate:**
- Industry potential (40 points)
- Stage appropriateness (30 points)
- Geographic market growth (30 points)

**Why it matters:**
Silicon Valley investors look for billion-dollar markets. Market size determines ceiling for growth.

**Scoring logic:**
- **Industry Potential**:
  - Fintech: 40 pts (1.8x valuation multiplier)
  - HealthTech/SaaS: 38 pts (1.6-1.7x multiplier)
  - EdTech/AgriTech/CleanTech: 35 pts (1.4-1.6x)
  - E-commerce/Logistics: 30 pts (1.3-1.4x)
  - Other: 20 pts (1.0x)
- **Stage**:
  - Scaling: 30 pts
  - Revenue: 25 pts
  - MVP: 20 pts
  - Prototype: 15 pts
  - Idea: 10 pts
- **Market Growth** (African markets):
  - Rwanda: 28% annual growth
  - Nigeria: 25% annual growth
  - Kenya: 22% annual growth
  - Ghana: 20% annual growth
  - Egypt: 18% annual growth
  - South Africa: 15% annual growth

---

### 5. Innovation (12% weight)

**What we evaluate:**
- Innovation keywords and signals (50 baseline + bonuses)
- Differentiation from existing solutions

**Why it matters:**
Peter Thiel asks "What important truth do very few people agree with you on?" True innovation creates 10x improvements, not 10% improvements.

**Scoring logic:**
- **Innovation Keywords** (+10 pts each):
  - AI, machine learning, blockchain
  - "Disrupting", "revolutionary", "first"
  - "Unique", "patent", "proprietary"
  - "Breakthrough", "novel"
- **Generic Penalty** (-5 pts each):
  - "We provide", "platform for"
  - Overly generic descriptions

---

### 6. African Impact (10% weight)

**What we evaluate:**
- African market focus (40 points)
- Scale of impact (35 points)
- Local founder advantage (25 points)

**Why it matters:**
Harambeans focuses on Africa-specific problems and opportunities. Local founders have context advantage and commitment to the continent.

**Scoring logic:**
- **African Focus** (+8 pts each keyword):
  - Geographic: Africa, Kenya, Nigeria, Ghana, Rwanda, etc.
  - Problem areas: Financial inclusion, unbanked, informal sector, rural, SMEs
- **Impact Scale** (+7 pts each):
  - Keywords: Million, thousands, communities, scale, mass
  - Social impact: Underserved, marginalized, employment, jobs
- **Local Founder**:
  - Operating in African country: 25 pts
  - Other: 10 pts (benefit of doubt)

---

### 7. Sustainability (10% weight)

**What we evaluate:**
- Revenue model clarity (40 points)
- Runway planning (30 points)
- Funding amount appropriateness (30 points)

**Why it matters:**
Harambeans values sustainable, long-term businesses over flash-in-the-pan hype. Can this business survive and thrive?

**Scoring logic:**
- **Revenue Model**:
  - Already generating revenue: 25 pts
  - Clear use of funds (100+ chars): 15 pts
- **Runway**:
  - 18+ months: 30 pts
  - 12-18 months: 25 pts
  - 6-12 months: 20 pts
  - <6 months: 10 pts
- **Funding Fit** (target: $15k):
  - $10k-$25k: 30 pts (perfect fit)
  - $5k-$50k: 20 pts (reasonable)
  - Outside range: 10 pts

## Valuation Engine

### Methodology

The valuation engine uses a multi-factor approach:

```
Current Valuation = Base Stage Valuation × Score Multiplier × Industry Multiplier × Market Stability
```

**Factors:**

1. **Base Stage Valuation**
   - Idea: $50k-$200k (avg $125k)
   - Prototype: $150k-$500k (avg $325k)
   - MVP: $300k-$800k (avg $550k)
   - Revenue: $500k-$2M (avg $1.25M)
   - Scaling: $1M-$5M (avg $3M)

2. **Score Multiplier**
   - Formula: 0.5 + (score / 100)
   - Range: 0.5x (score 0) to 1.5x (score 100)
   - Example: Score 75 → 1.25x multiplier

3. **Industry Multiplier**
   - Fintech: 1.8x
   - SaaS: 1.7x
   - HealthTech: 1.6x
   - AgriTech/CleanTech: 1.5-1.6x
   - E-commerce/Logistics: 1.3-1.4x
   - Other: 1.0x

4. **Market Stability Factor**
   - Rwanda: 0.95
   - South Africa: 0.92
   - Kenya: 0.90
   - Ghana: 0.88
   - Nigeria: 0.85
   - Egypt: 0.82

### Projections (3-5 Year)

**Growth Multiplier Calculation:**
```
Base Multiplier = 5x (for average startups)

Adjustments:
- Score 80+: 10x
- Score 70-79: 8x
- Score 60-69: 6x
- Score <60: 5x

Stage Multipliers:
- Scaling: 1.5x
- Revenue: 1.3x
- MVP: 1.1x

Industry Boost:
- Fintech/SaaS/HealthTech: 1.2x
```

**3-Year Projection:**
```
Valuation_3yr = Current Valuation × Growth Multiplier × (Market Growth Rate ^ 3)
```

**5-Year Projection:**
```
Valuation_5yr = Current Valuation × (Growth Multiplier × 1.5) × (Market Growth Rate ^ 5)
```

### Example Calculation

**Scenario:**
- Company: Nigerian FinTech
- Stage: MVP
- Score: 78/100
- Industry: Fintech
- Market: Nigeria (25% growth, 0.85 stability)

**Current Valuation:**
```
Base = $550k (MVP average)
Score Multiplier = 0.5 + 0.78 = 1.28
Industry = 1.8x
Stability = 0.85

Current = $550k × 1.28 × 1.8 × 0.85 = $1,078,080
```

**3-Year Projection:**
```
Growth Multiplier = 8 (score 78) × 1.1 (MVP) × 1.2 (Fintech) = 10.56
Market Growth = 1.25^3 = 1.95

3-Year = $1.08M × 10.56 × 1.95 = $22.2M
```

**5-Year Projection:**
```
Growth Multiplier = 10.56 × 1.5 = 15.84
Market Growth = 1.25^5 = 3.05

5-Year = $1.08M × 15.84 × 3.05 = $52.2M
```

|-------------|--------|----------------|

## Output Format

The system generates a comprehensive report including:

### 1. Overall Score
- Numerical score (0-100)
- Rating label
- Investment recommendation

### 2. Category Breakdown
- Individual scores for all 7 categories
- Visual representation (progress bars)
- Weighted contribution to total score

### 3. Valuation Analysis
- Current valuation with breakdown
- 3-year projection with assumptions
- 5-year projection with assumptions

### 4. Qualitative Analysis
- **Strengths**: Top 3 performing categories with descriptions
- **Concerns**: Areas scoring below 50 with improvement suggestions

### 5. Next Steps
- Customized action items based on score tier
- Timeline expectations
- Required follow-ups

## Integration

### API Usage

**Submit Application:**
```javascript
const formData = new FormData();
formData.append('founderName', 'Jane Doe');
formData.append('email', 'jane@startup.com');
```

## Limitations & Disclaimers

1. **Automated Scoring**: While comprehensive, automated scoring should complement (not replace) human judgment
2. **Market Volatility**: Valuations assume stable market conditions
3. **Data Quality**: Scoring accuracy depends on applicant data quality
4. **Context Matters**: Qualitative factors may not be captured fully
5. **Not Financial Advice**: Valuations are estimates, not guarantees
